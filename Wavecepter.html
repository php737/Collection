How to Use

1

Select Station

Choose the transmitter matching your watch brand. Casio China → BPC, Japan → JJY.

2

Prepare Audio

Set volume to 100%. Disable all audio enhancements and equalizers.

3

Couple Signal

Wrap wired earphone cable around the watch 2-3 turns near the antenna position.

4

Start Receiving

Set watch to manual receive mode (RC/RX/GET). Keep still for 2-6 minutes.
